ABOUT HUBCORE VIBES

One universe.
Endless connections.

HubCore Vibes is a South African-founded digital platform in development. Its long-term vision is to connect community, creators, entertainment, gaming, discovery, commerce and future everyday digital experiences inside one evolving ecosystem.

Explore the live platformJoin Early Access

What HubCore is building

Community-first

HubCore is being developed around people, conversations, creator content and personalised discovery rather than a feed dominated by advertising.

Connected experiences

The wider HubCore vision includes VibeFeed, Vibes Chat, HubBeats, VibeFlix, HubPlay, VibeShop, HubVentures and VibeGo.

Built in South Africa

The platform is founded and developed by Yutani Pretorius, with the current public site acting as a live early-stage product and community prototype.
