Copyright & Content Reporting

Early Access · Last updated 16 September 2026

Creators should control their work.

If you upload media to HubCore Vibes, you must own it or have permission to use it.

Report copyright concerns

Email hubcore-vibes@outlook.com with the subject Copyright Report. Include your name and contact details, identification of the work you believe is protected, the HubCore URL or enough information to locate the material, why you believe the use is unauthorised, and confirmation that the information you provide is accurate.

Other content concerns

Use the same contact address for impersonation, privacy, scam or safety reports and describe the content and concern clearly. Reports are reviewed during Early Access and moderation tooling will continue to expand.

Good-faith reports

Please do not submit false or abusive reports. A report may affect another person's content or account, so provide accurate information and only report material you genuinely believe requires review.
