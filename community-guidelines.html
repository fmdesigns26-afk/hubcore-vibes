Community Guidelines

Early Access · Last updated 16 September 2026

Keep the vibe human.

HubCore Vibes is being built around community, creativity and discovery. Participate as a real person, respect other people and help create a space people want to return to.

What belongs here

Original thoughts, conversations, feedback, creative work, respectful debate, community support and content you have the right to share.

What does not belong here

Do not post targeted harassment, threats, hateful attacks, sexual exploitation, scams, impersonation, malicious links, spam, unlawful content, or private personal information about another person without permission.

Promotion and business content

Organic Community and Reel Vibes should stay community-first. Paid or dedicated commercial discovery belongs in promoteVibe when that feature opens. Repetitive unsolicited advertising may be removed from organic spaces.

Media and copyright

Only upload photos, video, music or other material you own or are authorised to use. See our Copyright & Reporting page if you believe your work has been used without permission.

Moderation

HubCore may remove content or restrict accounts when necessary to protect users, the platform or legal rights. Early Access moderation systems will continue evolving as the community grows.

Report a concern

During Early Access, concerns can be sent to hubcore-vibes@outlook.com. Include enough information for the content to be located and reviewed.
